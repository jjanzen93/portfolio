**Student Project** 

Pinballistic is a pinball roguelite that is currently in production, set to release in Winter 2026. To view the gameplay trailer, head to our [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4494280/Pinballistic/).

Developed as a senior capstone project within a collaborative team of five, the project represents a comprehensive journey through the full production lifecycle—from initial ideation to a market-ready Steam release. The game was recently awarded 1st Place at the CMCA Awards (a class wide competition for vertical slices), distinguishing it as the top project among over 40 competing titles for its polish and exceptional game feel. I’ve also been accepted into the exclusive last class in the series, Game Production Studio, where teams take their game from a vertical slice to a demo or fully playable game.

From a technical perspective, my primary responsibility is audio, both music and SFX creation and implementation. I strive to create a suite of sound effects that is cohesive with the overall aesthetic of the game from visuals to mechanics. My main goal is to reinforce tactile elements through sound, creating affordances that cultivate a more intuitive experience. Additionally, as we’ve moved past the vertical slice and toward demo/beta territory, I’ve been heavily involved with item design and increasing replayability. In this vein, I suggested and set the design team up with Obsidian for tracking item synergies and keywords, created a plan to introduce a difficulty system, and outlined our path toward more procedural generation in player goals from run to run.

The Game Studio courses at UCSC have provided a structure for me to work on Pinballistic as a part of the curriculum, and primarily ask for deliverables that would be important or useful to have even without a grade attached to them, like style guides, gameplay pillars, playtest feedback forms, and check-in presentations to showcase projects. In this structure, the work is managed through an Agile workflow of sprints and milestones, which give us the opportunity to create time-based and attainable goals in weekly meetings.
