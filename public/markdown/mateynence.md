**Game Jam** 

[Mateynence](https://noyke.itch.io/matey-nence) was created by myself and Nicholas Lafredo for the 30 Days of Summer Jam in July 2025, of which the task was to create a game with high scores and a summer feel. In addition to some positive feedback, our game inspired another designer to create their own minigame collection for a later jam.

My work on Mateynence was as a *Jack*-of-all-trades (no pun intended): ideation, gameplay programming, sound implementation, and more were my responsibility. In such a short period of time, it was crucial that tasks be actionable and specific, and that I took all steps necessary to streamline my workflow. Keeping the minigame system modular was a great way to do that, instructing a manager singleton instance to access an array of scene references that we could add and remove from. This made testing and adding new minigames much easier. Additionally, balancing fast-paced gameplay with the steep learning curve of nine games meant that centralized balance control(like having minigame timers and their difficulty scaling in one place) was incredibly important for adjusting to playtester feedback.

During the development of Mateynence, I gained a solid foundation for working with Unity, as I had only worked with the engine a handful of times before this. As such, I learned many lessons, both in proficiency with the engine as well as some more useful features like FMOD integration. From a design perspective, I learned the importance of an intuitive user interface (this would have greatly lessened the aforementioned learning curve) and how to keep the player entertained through auditory feedback. Having a voice shouting commands both added to the immersion of working on a pirate ship and gave players a sense of urgency to complete the task in front of them.

Ultimately, the project proved that even within tight constraints, a focus on intuitive audio and modular systems can create something cohesive and fun. This was a great jumping off point for the more complex projects I'm working on today.
